# RACK FIVE — Quick Start Guide

A word game played like Yahtzee. Build words instead of rolling dice.

---

## The Idea

You get a rack of letter tiles instead of five dice. Build **five words** each turn. A word's **length** becomes its **rank**, and rank plays exactly like a die face.

| Word length | Rank |
|---|---:|
| 3 letters | 1 |
| 4 letters | 2 |
| 5 letters | 3 |
| 6 letters | 4 |
| 7 letters | 5 |
| 8+ letters | 6 |

Three of a kind = three words of equal length. A straight = words of consecutive lengths. Same Yahtzee logic, new engine.

---

## Setup

- **100-tile letter bag** (standard word-game distribution, includes 2 blanks)
- **Scorecard** with 13 categories
- Pick a dictionary (e.g. TWL or SOWPODS) and stick with it

**Pick a difficulty** — this sets your letter budget:

| Difficulty | Budget | Grid |
|---|---:|---:|
| Easy | 45 | 5×9 |
| **Medium (default)** | **40** | **5×8** |
| Hard | 35 | 5×7 |

Your budget = loose tiles + letters already used in words, always. Hard blocks only one thing: a rank-6 (all 8-letter) Rack Five. Everything else is reachable on any difficulty.

---

## How a Turn Works

1. **Draw.** All tiles return to the bag and remix. Draw your full budget of letters face up.
2. **Build.** Form valid words (3+ letters) into your five word slots using loose tiles. Words are **provisional** — rearrange or dismantle them freely, no penalty.
3. **Refresh (optional, twice per turn).** Discard up to 10 loose tiles and draw replacements. **Refreshing locks in every word you've placed as permanent** — this is your commitment point, so refresh only when you're ready to stop rearranging.
4. **Score.** Fill all five slots, convert word lengths to ranks, and enter the result into one open scorecard category. Scoring also locks in any remaining provisional words.

Play 13 turns, one per scorecard category. If a slot is still empty after your final refresh, it scores as **rank 0**.

### Word Rules
- 3-letter minimum, no maximum length
- No exact word twice in the same hand (EAT and ATE are fine together)
- The same word can reappear in a later turn — no tracking needed across the game

---

## Scoring

Your final score has three parts:

**1. Scorecard** — standard Yahtzee-style scoring on ranks (see table below).

**2. Word Bank** — the single highest-value word in each hand (by letter tile value; blanks = 0) gets added to a running bank. Only one word per turn counts, so concentrate your best letters into one "money word" and don't worry about the rest.

**3. Jumbo bonus** — the *first* word you ever play that's 9+ letters earns a one-time bonus of 3× its length.

---

## The Scorecard

**Upper Section** — score = (points per word) × (number of words of that length)

| Category | Word length | Points/word |
|---|---|---:|
| Ones | 3 letters | 1 |
| Twos | 4 letters | 2 |
| Threes | 5 letters | 3 |
| Fours | 6 letters | 4 |
| Fives | 7 letters | 5 |
| Sixes | 8+ letters | 6 |

Score 63+ across the upper section for a **+35 bonus**.

**Lower Section**

| Category | Requirement | Score |
|---|---|---:|
| Three of a Kind | 3+ words of one rank | Sum of all 5 ranks |
| Four of a Kind | 4+ words of one rank | Sum of all 5 ranks |
| Full House | exactly two ranks, split 3-and-2 | 25 |
| Small Straight | 4 distinct consecutive ranks | 30 |
| Large Straight | 5 distinct consecutive ranks | 40 |
| Chance | anything | Sum of all 5 ranks |
| Rack Five | all 5 words the same rank | 10 × rank |

**Notes:**
- A Rack Five does *not* count for Full House (only one distinct rank, not two).
- Rank 0 (an empty slot) never counts toward any pattern — no straights, no sets, no full house.
- If nothing fits, take a zero somewhere. That's part of the game.

## Timing Options

| Mode | Time per turn |
|---|---|
| Relaxed | Untimed |
| Standard | 3 minutes |
| Blitz | 90 seconds |

At the buzzer, any unfilled slot becomes rank 0.

---

## Hints

Stuck? A hint costs **3 points off your Word Bank** and reveals one valid word of a length you choose from your current loose tiles. Max 2 hints per game. Your Word Bank can't go negative.

---

## The Big Decision, Every Turn

> Do I build the best word I can — or the exact word length my scorecard needs?

That tension is the whole game. Have fun.
